## Q5 — Browser / no-build distribution

- **The published tarball (arquero-8.0.3.tgz, 360 files, 2.73 MB unpacked) ships exactly three runtime dist artifacts plus types: `dist/arquero.js` (759,244 bytes), `dist/arquero.min.js` (236,290 bytes), `dist/arquero.min.js.map` (1,144,357 bytes), and `dist/types/**/*.d.ts`.** `source:` https://registry.npmjs.org/arquero (tarball inspected) · `publisher:` npm registry · `pub_date:` 2025-05-29 · `accessed:` 2026-08-01 · `confidence:` high · `class:` packaging
- **Both dist bundles are UMD, not ESM — the minified file begins `!function(t,e){"object"==typeof exports&&"undefined"!=typeof module?e(exports):"function"==typeof define&&define.amd?define(["exports"],e):e((t=…globalThis…).aq={})…`, i.e. a script tag yields the global `aq`.** `source:` dist/arquero.min.js in arquero-8.0.3.tgz · `publisher:` uwdata · `pub_date:` 2025-05-29 · `accessed:` 2026-08-01 · `confidence:` high · `class:` packaging
- **package.json points `unpkg` and `jsdelivr` at `./dist/arquero.min.js`, while `main` and `module` both point at `./src/index.js` (`"type": "module"`, no `exports` map). So a CDN script tag serves the UMD bundle, whereas an ESM CDN (esm.sh / jspm / Skypack) resolves to the raw ESM source tree.** `source:` https://registry.npmjs.org/arquero · `publisher:` npm registry · `pub_date:` 2025-05-29 · `accessed:` 2026-08-01 · `confidence:` high · `class:` packaging

## Leads

- Verify what is actually driving the 10x download growth — plausible candidates are AI-eval tooling (@meridianlabs/inspect-scout-viewer, METR/hawk) and generated-app scaffolding, but per-package volumes do not add up to 249k/mo, so a large share may be CI mirrors or LLM-generated project templates. A dependents crawl or npm's per-version download split would settle it.
- Contact path if a fix is ever needed: jheer's #361 comment offers to guide a contributor. A well-scoped PR with tests is the cheapest hedge before forking.
- Mosaic + DuckDB-WASM is the UW IDL forward path if the project ever outgrows in-memory transformation; it is not API-compatible and implies a WASM payload, which conflicts with a single-file browser tool.
- @uwdata/flechette (last release 2026-05-07) is the live half of Arquero's dependency pair; watch it, since a breaking flechette 3.x would be the most likely thing to strand Arquero 8.0.3.
- Consider a lockfile-pinned vendored copy of the repo (11k LOC, green tests) as the concrete hedge — cost measured, not speculative.
